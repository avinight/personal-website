Problem-Solving Strategies
0. Tree diagrams: Tree diagrams are intuitive and useful tools for finding probabilities of events that can be ordered sequentially.

2. Conditioning: Given an event A for which we want to find P(A), introducing disjoint events B1 , . . . , Bk and applying the law of total probability, whereby the conditional probabilities P(A|Bi) are easier and more natural to solve than P(A).

3. Hypothetical tables: Hypothetical tables can be constructed for many scenarios involving probabilities and can be used in many of the same situations as tree diagrams.
