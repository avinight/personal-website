##### Proposition
---
For $T : V \to  W$ a linear map,
1) $Ran(T) \subseteq W$ is a subspace
2) $null(T) \subseteq V$ is a subspace

Proof. 
1) Closed under +
Take $w_1, w_2 \in Ran(T)$. WTS $w_1 + w_2 \in Ran(T)$

Know there is $v_1 \in V$ such that 
$T(v_1) = w_1, \ex v_2 \in V$ such that $T(v_2) = w_2$

Then $T(v_1 + v_2) = T(v_1) + T(v_2) = w_1 + w_2$
Therefore $w_1 + w_2 \in Ran(T)$

2) Closed under $\cdot$
Take $w \in Ran(T)$, $\ex v \in V$ such that $T(v) = w$. 
If $\lambda \in \mathbb{F}$, then WTS $\lambda w \in Ran(T)$
$T(\lambda v) = \lambda T(v) = \lambda w$
