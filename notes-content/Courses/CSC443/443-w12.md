# Detect and prevent deadlocks
Abort a transaction after waiting a certain time for a lock
- Pros? Simple
- Cons? Speculative - wastes work aborting on non-deadlocks

Conservative two phase locking
- Pros: no deadlocks
- Con: takes more locks and holds them for longer**

Abort on wait
- **Pros: no deadlocks**
- **Con: defeatist** (wastes work, or aborts many transactions)

Cycle detection
- We detect a deadlock for sure, other approaches are speculative
- Complex, computational overhead. We have to traverse the graph
- Abort the transaction that 
	- has done the least work, furthest from completion, aborted the least times, most transactions waiting on it
