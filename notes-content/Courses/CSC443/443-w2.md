Random might evict frequently accessed pages
FIFO might leave pages in frames we want to insert into, since the oldest evicted isn't the one we want to insert into.
FIFO also turns a hash table buffer pool into an array. We want to access buffer pool in constant time, not having to do a linear search/probe to find where the new page fits.

You can use the `pin_count` for clock or no (generalized clock)

metadata with count doesn't work because of the flaw where if you access a page once and you want to access it more times, it may be evicted earlier due to the policy "lowest count gets evicted"

worst case scenario of clock, all pages are hot, all are accessed frequently and we can't evict.
