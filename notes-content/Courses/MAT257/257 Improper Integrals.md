### Definitions
---
- (Extended integral)

```ad-note
title: Convention

If $A$ is an open set in $\mathbb{R}^n$, then $\int_A f$ will denote the extended integral unless specifically stated otherwise.

```

Lemma 15.1
^0e3bb9
```ad-note
title: Lemma 15.1

Let $A$ be an open set in $\mathbb{R}^n$. Then there exists a sequence $C_1, C_2, ...$ of compact rectifiable subsets of $A$ whose union is $A$, such that $C_N \subset \texttt{Int} \space C_{N+1}$ for each $N$.

```
This lemma is used a lot.
