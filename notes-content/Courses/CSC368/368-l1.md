#### Goals
1. List and describe important trends, laws, and inflection points in computer architecture, like: <span style="background:#b1ffff">Moore's Law, Dennard Scaling, the power wall</span>
2. Differentiate between <span style="background:#b1ffff">instruction-, thread-, and data-level parallelism</span>
3. Sketch and describe block diagrams of high-level architectures and concepts, like a <span style="background:#b1ffff">multi-core processor, vector processor, and the memory hierarchy</span>
4. Compare and contrast instruction set architectures along several dimensions, including their design philosophy and operand model
