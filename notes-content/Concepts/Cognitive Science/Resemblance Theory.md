## Tversky?

He came up with a featural formula:

$$\text{sim}(I, J) = \text{af}(I \cap J) - \text{bf}(I - J)- \text{cf}(J - I)$$

f = how salient (perceptual: HOW DO THEY STAND OUT TO YOU) those features are (perceptual judgements of [[Relevance]]) Useful for human beings not useful for us trying to make a machine that has intelligence.
