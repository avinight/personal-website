# Vocabulary
## Interesting
- #tabulate
	- arrange (data) in tabular form.
- #gesticulate
	- use gestures, especially dramatic ones, instead of speaking or to emphasize one's words.
- #stipulate
	- demand or specify (a requirement), typically as part of a bargain or agreement.
- #anthropic
	- involving or concerning the existence of human life, especially as a constraint on theories of the universe.
- #sophistry
	- the use of fallacious arguments, especially with the intention of deceiving.
- #segue
	- often confused with segway
	- an uninterrupted transition from one piece of music or film scene to another.
- #ineffable
	- too great or extreme to be expressed or described in words.
- #disconcerting
	- causing one to feel unsettled.
