# What is a heuristic?
A problem solving technique that increases your chances of finding a solution.

They deal with [[Combinatorial Explosion]] by pre-specifying what you should find salient, relevant to you.

Prejudged, predujiced, biased



## representativeness heuristic

- Treat something from a class of things => how representative it is of the category to which it belongs
- prototype effects
- generalize to the future

[[Memory#^fa9459 | the loftus]] of crash vs collide
	- representative of a disastrous event

## availability heuristic

- judge how probable an event is by how easily i can remember a similar event or available it is to my imagination

Every heuristic has an irremovable vulnerability to bias; we are always suseptible to it. ^920978
