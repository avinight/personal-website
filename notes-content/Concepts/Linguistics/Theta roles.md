# Theta Roles

Agent: The initiator or doer of an action.
- My notebook climbed the ladder
	- Notebooks aren't sentient -> Not a valid agent

Experiencer: Arguments that feel or perceive events.
- Joss likes shiba inus
	- likes takes experiencer as a subject
- Melany saw the performance
	- "see" and "heard" are passive actions => experiencer
	- "looked at" is an active action => agent

Theme: Entities that undergo actions, are moved, experienced, or perceived.
- They're the objects of the sentence
	- The tigers ate the lamb => "lamb" is the thing that is being ate
	- This key opens the door => "door" is the thing being opened
	- Many people fear spiders => "many people" is an experiencer, "spiders" is the thing being feared

Goal: Entity towards which motion takes place.
- I went to Disneyland.
	- Disneyland is the goal
- I gave my love to Walt
	- To Walt is the goal (abstract)

Location: Place where an action occurs.
- We live in California
	- California is the place being lived in
