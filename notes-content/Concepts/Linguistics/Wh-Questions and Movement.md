# Wh-Questions and movement

Cindy ate a pie. __Declarative__
Cindy ate what. __Wh-in-situ__ or __Echo Question__
What did Cindy eat \_? __Wh-movement__

When replaces AdvPs
Where replaces PPs

Echo questions take up position of DP and there is no movement.

Moving out of a phase requires you to be in (Spec, CP) position.

Island Constraints
- The subject condition
	- Wh-movement is not possible outside of subjects in English!
	- *Who should that Mary hired have surprised you?
	- That Mary hired WHO should have surprised you
- Wh-island movement
	- Don't extract a wh-word outside a wh-island!
	- *Which house did Sally ask who bought?
	- Sally asked who bought WHAT?
- Complex DP
	-  Don’t move a wh-word from inside a CP that is inside a DP!
	-  Relative clauses prevent wh-movement outside of them
- Coordination Structure
	- Don't extract a wh-word outside a coordinate structure!
	- *What did your brother buy a guitar and sell?
	- Your brother bought a guitar and sold WHAT?

- Contraint not discussed
	- *Whose does the teacher like essay best?
	- The teacher likes WHOSE essay best?
