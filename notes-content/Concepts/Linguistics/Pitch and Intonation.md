# Pitch and Intonation

Pitch: The quality we hear in playing two different notes.
Intonation: the use of pitch to mark grammatical and emotional meaning of an utterance.
Intonation Contour: The pitch pattern for an utternace.
- Mark the tonic syllable with X at the beginning of it (above)
- For statements, apply a falling curve, breaking it up before the tonic syllable (unless it is the first syllable)
- For yes/no questions or lists, apply a rising curve, breaking it up the same way.
- When more than 1 intonational phrase, add boundaries (so that 1 tonic syllable per phrase)
