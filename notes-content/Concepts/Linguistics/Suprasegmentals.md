# Suprasegmentals

Suprasegmental (or **prosodic**) features are phonological properties that extend over more than a single segment (vowel or consonant) — they are layered *on top of* the segmental string rather than belonging to one phone.

The main suprasegmentals are:

- **Stress** — relative prominence of a syllable.
- **Tone / pitch** — see [[Pitch and Intonation]].
- **Intonation** — pitch contour over a phrase or sentence.
- **Length / quantity** — see [[Vowel Length]].

For the concrete English inventory, see [[English Suprasegmentals]].
