# Airstream Mechanisms

Case study: punjabi

Egressive: flowing out of the body
Ingressive: Flowing into the body

pulmonic: with the lungs
glottalic: by raising or lowering the glottis
velaric: with air trapped in front of a dorsovelar closure

Positive [+] pressure: is when pressure in lungs, pharynx, and mouth is higher than outside air pressure.

Negative [-] pressure: is when pressure is lower than outside air pressure.

Ejectives: glottalic egressive stops -> Stop with a stronger burst

Implosives (usually voiced): downward moving larynx

Korean fortis (tense) stops: produced with a tightly constricted (and optionally raised larynx); a weak burst 

Velaric ingressive: 
the tongue makes a dorso-velar closure, with a simlutaneous more anterior closure;
the tongue is pulled back/low, decreasing air pressure at the front.

Velaric egressive:
the same as ingressive, but the tongue moves forward/up, increasing air pressure.
