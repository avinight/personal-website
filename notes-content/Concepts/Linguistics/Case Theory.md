# Case Theory

What does it assign?
NOMinative Case: is assigned in the specifier of finite T.
ACCusative Case: is assigned as the sister to transitive V.
PREPositional Case: Assigned to the sister of a preposition.

Case Filter Hypothesis
1) All DPs must get case.
2) If a DP does not get case, then there is a crash

Why is "I killed they" ungrammatical?
	'kill' gives ACC to DP 3. PL => Them
