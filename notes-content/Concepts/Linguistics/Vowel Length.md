# Vowel Length

Inherent length:  
- some vowels (tense vowels, diphthongs) are inherently longer in duration than others(lax vowels)

Contextual length: 
- vowels have a longer duration in certain phonological environments (open syllables, before voiced consonants) than in others (before voiceless consonants)

Lax vowels have no inherent length.
Tense vowels have inherent length.
Open and voiced vowels have contextual length
Closed and Voiceless vowels have no contextual length

/ə/ schwa is an exceptional lax vowel
