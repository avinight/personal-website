# Modus Tollens

A [[Rule of inference]]

[[Truth Table]]

$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
  \hline
  p & q & p \implies q & \lnot q & \lnot p\\
  \hline
  T & T & T \\
  \hline 
  T & F & F \\
  \hline 
  F & T & T \\
  \hline 
  F & F & T & T & T\\
  \hline 
\end{array}
$$
