# Tautology

A tautology is a formula that is True for every possible assignment of values to its propositional variables. Decide if each of the following propositional formulas are tautologies.

$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
p & \lnot p & p \lor \lnot p \\
\hline
T & F & T \\
\hline
F & T & T \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$
