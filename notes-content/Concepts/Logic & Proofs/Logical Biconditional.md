# Logical Biconditional

Two directions
$p \iff q$ means $(p \implies q) \land (q \implies p)$

Two things have the same value.
- Both sides are True or both sides are False

[[Truth Table]]
$$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
p & q & p \implies q & q \implies p & p \iff q \\
\hline
T & T & T & T & T \\
\hline
T & F & F & T & F \\
\hline
F & T & T & F & F \\
\hline
F & F & T & T & T \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$
