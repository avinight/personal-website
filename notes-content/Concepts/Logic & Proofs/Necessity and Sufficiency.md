# Necessity and Sufficiency
r is a necessary and sufficient condition for s 
- $r \iff s$

# Necessity
necessary ^c8a579
- Something that must be present for another condition to occur

r is a necessary condition for s
- $\lnot r \implies \lnot s$
- $s \implies r$

# Sufficiency
sufficient 
- the thing that produces the condition 
^c87178

r is a sufficient condition for s
- $\lnot s \implies \lnot r$
- $r \implies s$
