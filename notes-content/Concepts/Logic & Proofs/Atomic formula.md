# Atomic Formula

Atomic formulas are 0-arity structure formulas with no deeper propositional structure.

Minimality of [[Prime Numbers]] can be viewed as prime numbers being "indecomposable"; that is, there are no factors k, and 1 < m < n such that n = km.

Atomic Numbers Predicate:
$\forall{a, b} \in \N, n \nmid a \land n \nmid b \implies n \nmid ab$, where $n \in \N$
