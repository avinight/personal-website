# Proof by Contradiction

Assume that negation $\lnot P$ is true, then use the assumption to prove $Q$ and $\lnot Q$, which is the contradiction that arises from the assumption that $\lnot P$ is true.

We argue the if P is false, then Q must be true, but its negation $\lnot Q$ must also be true.

But these two things can't be true at the same time.
