A card is drawn from a standard deck. Let $A$ be the event that it is a spade. Let B be the event that it is an ace. Then,
$$P(A|B) = \frac{P(AB)}{P(B)} = \frac{P(\mbox{Ace of Spades})}{P(\mbox{Ace})} = \frac{1/52}{1/13} = \frac{1}{4} = P(A)
$$
The events A and B are not mutually exclusive since AB = {Ace of Spades} $\neq \emptyset$
