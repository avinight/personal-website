```ad-note 
title: Axiom (Law of Total Probability LTP)
color: 223, 22, 223

If $B_1,B_2,...,B_k$ is a collection of mutually exclusive (disjoint) and exhaustive events that partition the [[Sample Space]], then for any event $A$
$$\begin{align*}P(A) &= P(A \cap B_1) + P(A \cap B_2) + \ldots + P(A \cap B_k) \\
&= P(A|B_1) \cdot P(B_1) + P(A|B_2) \cdot P(B_2) + \ldots + P(A|B_k) \cdot P(B_k)\\
&= \sum_{i = 1}^k P(A|B_i) \cdot P(B_i)
\end{align*}
```
