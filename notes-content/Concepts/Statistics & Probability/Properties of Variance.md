# Properties of Variance

For any contant $a, b$ and discrete random variable $X$, the following are true (and easily proven):

- $V(a) = 0$
- $v(a + X) = V(X) = \sigma^2$
- $V(aX) = a^2 \cdot V(X) = a^2 \cdot \sigma^2$
- $V(aX + b) = a^2 \cdot V(X) = a^2 \cdot \sigma^2$ 
- (If x & y are independent): $V(X + Y) = V(X) + V(Y)$
- $V(X + Y) \neq V(X) + V(Y)$
