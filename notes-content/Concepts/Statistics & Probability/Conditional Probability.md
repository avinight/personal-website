```ad-note 
title: Definition

Consider a [[Random Experiment]] with [[Sample Space]] $\Omega$. Let $B$ be an [[Event (Probability)|event]] $(B \subseteq \Omega)$ with $P(B) > 0$. Let $b$ denote the elements of event $B$. Then,
1) $P(b|B) \geq 0$ for all $b \in B$
2) $\sum_{b \in B} P(b|B) = 1$ Sum of all elements in $b$ occuring when $B$ occurs
3) For $A \subseteq B$, $P(A|B) = \sum_{b \in A} P(b|B)$
```
