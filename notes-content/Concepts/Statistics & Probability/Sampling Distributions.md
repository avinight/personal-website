# Sampling Distributions

1.  When measurements are random values that follow a normal distribution, the probability distribution of sample means (average of data) is also a normal distribution
    
2.  The mean of the normal probability distribution of the sample means is the same as the mean of the probability distribution of the individual measurements.
    
3.  The standard deviation of the probability distribution of the sample means is smaller than the standard deviation of the probability distribution of the individual observations.
    
    If there are n values in the random sample and $\sigma$ is the standard deviation of the probability distribution of the individual observations, the standard deviation of the probability distribution of sample means is $\sigma/\sqrt{n}$
    
-   Central Limit Theorem
    
    As more and more measurements are taken, the probability distribution for the possible averages of the measurements will converge to a Normal distribution.
