# Statistical Tests

Conditions

-   10% condition (To measure independence)
    
    Sample must be no greater than 10% of the population to be independent
    
-   Success-Failure Condition (To measure normality)
    
    For CLT to hold:
    
    -   np ≥ 10
    -   n(1-p) ≥ 10
