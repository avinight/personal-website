# Divisibility

Let $n, d \in \mathbb{Z}$. We say that $d$ divides $n$, or $n$ is divisible by $d$, iff there exists a $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that $n = dk$

Insights
	- $n = dk$ means $k \divides n$ or $d \divides n$

A number $n = abcd$ is divisible by 2 exactly when d is one of 0, 2, 4, 6, 8. 
n is divisible by 5 if d is either 0 or 5.
