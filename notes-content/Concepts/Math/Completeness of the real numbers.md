## The least upper bound property
```ad-note
title: Axiom
color: 223, 22, 223

An [[Ordered Field]] F is <u>complete</u> if and only if 
- for every $A \subset F$, 
- $A \neq \emptyset$ such that 
- $A$ has an upper bound, 

Then there is a least upper bound $\mbox{sup}(A)$ for $A$ in $F$.
```
