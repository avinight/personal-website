# Determinants

The determinant is multiplicative with respect to matrix multiplication

The determinant of a similar matrix is 
$\text{det}(D) = \text{det}(PAP^{-1}) = \text{det}(P)\text{det}(A)\text{det}(P^{-1}) = \text{det}(P)\text{det}(A)\frac{1}{\text{det}(A)} =\text{det}(A)$

The determinant of a diagonal matrix is the diagonal
$\text{det}(D) = (1)(2)(3)(4) = 24$
