# Set Partitions
Let $A$ be a set. A (finite or infinite) collection of non-empty sets $\{A_1, A_2, \cdots\}$ is called a partition of $A$ when 


(1) Exhaustive: $A$ is the union of all of the $A_i$ ^a2d4ca

(2) [[Mutually exclusive]]: the sets $\{A_1, A_2, \cdots\}$ do not have any element in common; 
- (pairwise disjoint or non-overlapping): no two distinct sets $A_i, A_j$ have any element in common.
