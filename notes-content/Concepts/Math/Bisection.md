# Perpendicular bisector

A perpendicular bisector of a line segment is a line that is perpendicular to the line segment and goes through the middle of the line segment.

 
 [[Straightedge and compass construction]]
 1. AB is a line, CD is its bisector
 2. Define two circles using radius AB and BA
 3. The intersection of these two circles are the points C and D and the line between them is the bisector

Proof
1. Label point of intersection of AB with CD as E.
2. Draw line segments AC, CB, BD, DA
3. Prove AE = EB and angle CEA is a right angle
4. All line segments have same length since their radii is size of AB.
5. ACD is congruent to BCD
6. Triangle ACE is congruent to BCE
7. Angle ACE = BCE
8. Therefore AE = EB
9. Angle AEC = BEC
10. Therefore each of them is 90 since both sum to 180
