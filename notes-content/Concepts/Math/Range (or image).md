```ad-note 
title: Definition

The set of vectors that T can output.
$$\text{range}(T) = \{\vec{y} \in W : \vec{y} = T(\vec{x}) \text{ for some } \vec{x} \in V \}$$
```

Rank
- The dimension of the range of T.

Usually the subspace spanning the column space.
Domain is usually the subspace spanning the row space.
