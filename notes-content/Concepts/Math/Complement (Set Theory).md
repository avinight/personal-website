# Complement (set theory)

$$ A^c = U \setminus A$$
Let $A$ and $U$ be sets, and assume that $A \subset U$. U is a larger set. The absolute complement of $A$ in $U$, denoted $A^c$ is defined to be the set of elements that are in $U$ but not $A$. $A^c$ depends on the choice of both $U$ and $A$


[[De Morgan's Laws]] apply since complements act as a negation operation on a set.

## Relative complement

Given sets X and Y, the union of these sets is defined as
$$X \setminus Y = \{x \in X \divides x \notin Y\}$$
