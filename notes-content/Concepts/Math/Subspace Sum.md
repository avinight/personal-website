# Subspace sum

(1.3.5) Definition.
$W_1 + W_2 = \{x \in V \divides x = x_1 + x_2 \text{ for some } x_1 \in W \land x_2 \in W_2\}$

- The set of vectors that can be built up from the vectors in $W_1 \land W_2$ by linear combinations.
- Conversely, $W_1 + W_2$ are precisely those vectors in V that can be broken down into the sum of a vector in $W_1$ and a vector in $W_2$.
