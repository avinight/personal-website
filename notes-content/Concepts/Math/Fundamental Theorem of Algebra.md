# Fundamental Theorem of Algebra

A [[Field (mathematics) |field]] $F$ is called _algebraically closed_ if every polynomial $p(z) = a_nz^n + \cdots + a_1z + a_0$ with $a_1 \in F$ and $a_n \neq 0$ has $n$ roots counted with multiplicity in $F$.
