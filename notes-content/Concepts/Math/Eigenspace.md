# Eigenspace

Let $T : V \rightarrow V$ be a linear mapping, and let $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$, denoted $E_{\lambda}$, is the set $$E_{\lambda} = \{x \in V \divides T(x) = \lambda x\}$$
