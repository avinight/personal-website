Smallest Natural Number that is a sum of (positive or negative) multiples of two numbers

Let $a$ and $b$ be arbitrary natural numbers, and assume at least one of them is non-zero. Then gcd(a, b) is the smallest positive integer such that there exist $p, q \in \mathbb{Z}$ with $\text{gcd}(a, b) = ap + bq$
