# Basis (linear algebra)

A basis for a subspace $\mathcal{V}$ is a [[Linear Independence | Linearly Independent]] set of vectors $\mathcal{B}$ so that $\text{span }\mathcal{B} = \mathcal{V}$

Theorem
-  Let V be a vector space that has a finite spanning set, and let 5 be a linearly independent subset of V. Then there exists a basis 5' of V, with 5 C S'.
