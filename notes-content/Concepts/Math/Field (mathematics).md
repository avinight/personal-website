 ```ad-note 
title: Definition

 A field is a set F with two operations, defined on ordered pairs of elements of F, called addition and multiplication. 
 
 - Addition assigns to the pair $x$ and $y \in F$ their sum. which is denoted by $x + y$.
 - Multiplication assigns to the pair $x$ and $y \in F$ their product, which is denoted by $x \cdot y$ or $xy$. These two operations must satisfy the following properties:
```

 Axioms:
 1. $+$ and $\times$ are [[Associative property | associative]].
 2. $+$ and $\times$ are [[Commutative property | commutative]].
 3. $+$ has an identity, denote $0_F$
 4. Every element of $F$ has an inverse for the operation $+$ denoted by $-r$
 5. $\times$ has an identity, denoted $1_F$, every $r \neq {0_F}$ of $F$ has an inverse for the operation $\times$ denoted by $r^{-1}$
 6. Every $r \neq \{0_F\}$ of F has an inverse for the operation $\times$ denoted $r^-1$
 7. The two operations are related by the [[Distributivity | distribution]] properties: $a \times (b + c) = a \times b + a \times c$ and $(a + b) \times c = a \times c + b \times c$ for all $a, b, c \in F$ 

[[Difference between a field and a vector space]]
 
 The natural numbers are not a field because 
 - the set does not contain an additive identity
 - the set does not contain an additive inverse
 - the set does not contain a multiplicative inverse
 
 The integers are not a field because
 - the set does not contain an multiplicative inverse
 
 $\{0, 1\}$ Does not have binary operations, 
 - 1 + 1 = 2 and 2 is not in F
 
 ### Real and Complex Numbers
 
  The [[Real Number|real numbers]] are a field. Though it is less obvious that the complex numbers are also a field.
  
 [[Complex Number|Complex numbers]] build a field containing $\mathbb{R}$ in which all polynomials have their reals

 Is $\mathbb{R} \subseteq \mathbb{C}$? No since C maps a pair of numbers $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}$
