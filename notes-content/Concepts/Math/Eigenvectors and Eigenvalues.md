# Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues

Eigenvalues only exist for square matrices

Every eigenvalue for a matrix is a root of some polynomial (the characteristic polynomial)

Zero cannot be the eigenvalue of any matrix.

A 2x2 matrix does not always have a real eigenvalue

A 3x2 matrix does not have an eigenvalue
