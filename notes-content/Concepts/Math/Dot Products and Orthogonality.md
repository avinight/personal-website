# Dot Product and Orthogonality

Dot Product
 - Let x and y be vectors, then their dot product 
 $$ (x \cdot y) = \sum_{i=1}^n x_i y_i = |x||y| \cos \theta = x^Ty $$
 - Dot product is the magnitude of the projection of x onto y multiplied by the magnitude of y.

Distance between two vectors:

$$ ||x-y|| = \sqrt{(x-y) \cdot (x-y)}$$
$$ 		   = \sqrt{(x \cdot x) - (x \cdot y) - (y \cdot x) + (y \cdot y)} $$
$$ 	 	   = \sqrt{||x||^2 + ||y||^2} $$
