# Complex Conjugate

A complex conjugate for some [[Complex Number]] $z$, denoted by $\bar{z}$, is equal in magnitude but opposite in sign

$\forall z \in \mathbb{C}, |z| = |\bar{z}|$

Insights:
- A complex number multiplied by its conjugate $$(a, -b)(a, b) = a^2 + b^2$$
- $\overline{z+w} = \bar{z} + \bar{w}$
- $\overline{zw} = \bar{z}\bar{w}$
- $\overline{\bar{z}} = z$
