Intuitions about the simplicity of the process come to the floor:

What is the answer to this problem? (THIS IS ALL A LIE!)
	- Sesame street explanation: one of these things is not like the other, three of these things are kind of the same.
	- Pattern-making → Seeing similarity in partial identity, not full identity.
	- We exclude things that are dissimilar and that's how we form categories.
	- Categories are presupposed and subconscious processes → That's how we do it!

What is the problem with this problem?
	- Many things are obvious to us!
	- We need to not assume obviousness to explain things
		- We don't do this in science

How does our brain find things obvious?
