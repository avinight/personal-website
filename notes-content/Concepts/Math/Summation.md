# Summation

## Summation Notation

We define the empty sum with identity 0 as:
$$ \forall j, k \in \mathbb{Z}, j > k \implies \sum_{i=j}^k f(i) = 0$$

Properties of Summation
- Not an error/undefined
- Always increments by +1
