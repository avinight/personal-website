Null spaces are closely related with row spaces. 

By definition,
$$
M \vec{x} = \begin{bmatrix} \vec{r}_1 \cdot \vec{x} \\ \vdots \\ \vec{r}_n \cdot \vec{x} \end{bmatrix}
$$

- Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a linear transformation and let M be a matrix for $\mathcal{T}$. Then $\text{nullity}(\mathcal{T})$ is equal to the number of free variable columns in rref(M)

- For a matrix A, we have $\text{rank}(A) = \text{rank}(A)^T$
