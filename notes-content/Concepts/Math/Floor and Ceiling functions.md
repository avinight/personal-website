# Floor and Ceiling Function

the floor of a number x, denoted ⌊x⌋, is the largest integer less than or equal to x.
[[More with floor extra practice]]
- floor of x can be used directly, unknown quantities are defined as variables
- 'floor of x is an integer' and 'floor of x is less than x' and 'every integer greater than or equal to x'
$$(\lfloor x \rfloor \in \mathbb{Z}) \land (\lfloor x \rfloor \leq x) \land (\forall k \in \mathbb{Z}, k \leq x \implies \lfloor x \rfloor \geq k)$$

the ceiling of x, denoted ⌈x⌉, to be the smallest integer that is greater than or equal to x.
