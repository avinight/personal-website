# Irrationality of sqrt(2)

The statement: sqrt(2) is irrational

there does not exist p, q such that 
$$\left(\frac{p}{q}\right)^2 = 2$$
Proof 1.
Suppose there is for a contradiction where $p, q$ are coprime.
$$p^2 = 2q^2$$
since $p^2$ is a multiple of 2, $p$ is a multiple of 2.
$$p^2 = 4k^2 = 2q^2$$
Since $p, q$ are coprime, $q$ is odd.
$$(2r)^2 = 2q^2$$
$$4r^2 = 2q^2$$
since $q^2$ is even, $q$ is even.
Contradiction

Proof 2.
$$\{p + q \alpha \divides p, q \in \mathbb{Q}\}$$
where $\alpha^2 = 2$
