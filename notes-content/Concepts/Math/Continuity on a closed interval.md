```ad-note 
title: Definition

Suppose $\mbox{dom}(f) \supset [a, b]$, then $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ if 
$f$ is continuous at $x, \fa x \in [a, b]$

Where, $f$ is continuous at $a$ means $\lim_{x \to a^+} f(x) = f(a)$
$f$ is continuous at $b$ means $\lim_{x \to b^-} f(x) = f(b)$
```
