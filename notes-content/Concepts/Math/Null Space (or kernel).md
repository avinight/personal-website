```ad-note 
title: Definition

$T : V \to W$ is a linear map, then
$$\text{null}(T) = \{ \vec{x} \in V : T \vec{x} = \vec{0} \}
$$
is the null-space 
```
- The set of vectors that get mapped to the zero vector under a linear transformation.

- $\text{null}(T) \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ is a subspace

Nullity
- The dimension of the null space of T.
