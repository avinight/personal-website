```ad-note 
title: Definition

A cycle for the self-map $f: X \to X$ is a subset $X \subseteq X$ of the form $$S = \{x, f(X), f(f(x)),...\}$ Iteration of self-map applied to a point $x$
```

Main result: Given a bijective self-map $f: X \to X$, obtain a partition of $X$ into cycles (cycle of length 1 = fixed point)

[Any bijection $f: X \to X$ of a finite set is a product of disjoint cycles]
