# Symbolization

Stylistic Variants and Symbolization:

Let p, q, r  be some propositions

AND: But, Although, However, In addition to, Moreover, Even though, Despite the fact that, Yet

OR: 
- Unless -> (p or q) = (not p => q) = (not q => p)

EXCLUSIVE OR: p or q and not(p and q) = not(p <=> q)

CONDITIONAL: 
- [[Necessity and Sufficiency]]
- only (reverses the direction of the conditional)

CASES:
- in that case -> (last thing said in english => r)
- former -> (first case said in english => r)
- latter -> (last case said in english => r)

NEGATION (De Morgan's Laws):
- Neither, nor -> not (p or q) = not p and not q
- Not both -> not (p and q) = not p or not q

NUMERICAL CLAIMS:
- At least 2: (p and q) or (p and r) or (r and q)
- Exactly 1: (p and not q and not r) or (not p and q and not r) or (not p and not q and r)
- Exactly 2: (not p and q and r) or (p and not q and r) or (p and q and not r)
- At most 1: (not p and not p and not r) or Exactly 1
- At most 2: (not p and not p and not r) or Exactly 1 or Exactly 2

Indirect method for "at most 2":
- not (all 3)
- not (at least 3)

Indirect method for "at least 2":
- not (at most 1)

RESTRICTIVE  and NON-RESTRICTIVE CLAUSES:
- restrictive: that
- non-restrictive: ,which \_, who, whom
