# Tense and Aspect

Participle
- -en, -ed, irregular

Gerund
- -ing

Perfect Aspect = HAVE + V_participle
- Present Perfect = HAVE_present + VP_participle
- Past Perfect = HAVE_preterite + VP_participle

Progressive Aspect = BE + V_gerund
- Present Progressive = BE_present + VP_gerund
- Past Progressive = BE_past + VP_gerund
