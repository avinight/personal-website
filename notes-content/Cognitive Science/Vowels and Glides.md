# Vowels and Glides

Primary and Secondary vowels differ by rounding
Rhotacized vowels have a lowered F3.

Examples of[[Diacritics]]
- Expanded [e̘] vs. Retracted [e̙] tongue root

Pharynx expands and contracts

Cardinal Vowels are produced at the extreme periphery of the vowel space and are _ to one another.
- Their purpose is to provide reference points in the description of vowels.
- Vowel systesm of particular languages: are transcribed by using the cardinal vowel symbol that is the closes to each vowel in a language. 
- See [[Vowel Formants]]

Glides are non-syllabic counterparts of vowels. Any vowel symbol can be made to represent a glide by using a subscript arch [e̯].
