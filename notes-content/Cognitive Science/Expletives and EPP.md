# Expletives and the Extended Projection Principle (EPP)

'It' does not have a theta role in 'It rained.' -> Expletive

If we cannot replace 'it' with a noun, it is an expletive.

"It" that has theta roles
"James loves it." Theme
"It loves James." Agent

EPP
- All clauses must have subjects and lexical information expressed at all levels

If there are no theta roles to give and verb doesn't assign theta roles, then insert "it" expletive into subject position. (Spec, TP)

Factives select CP complement but do not assign theta roles.
