VP Internal Subject Hypothesis
- The external argument is within the VP!
	- D-structure: satisfies [[Selection and Auxiliaries#^e57d0d | Projection Principle B]]
	- S-structure: satisfies [[Selection and Auxiliaries#^d4d0d3 | EPP]]
	- If VP has theta roles, then VPISH holds.

An argument satisfying the Projection Principles ("gets its theta role") is distinct from the position in which it surfaces.

Phrasal movement always moves a phrase to a higher, open specifier position. 

Head movement always moves heads to a higher head position ^bc6e7c

Passive formation rules
	a. make the V [FORM participle]
	b. rob the verb of:
		i. its accusative case feature (so it can no longer check [ACC] on DP complements)
		ii. its ability to select an external argument
	c. make the VP a complement of the auxililary verb be.
