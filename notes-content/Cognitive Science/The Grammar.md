# The Grammar

Separate PSRs miss significant linguistic generalizations

[[Endocentricity]]
- Every phrase has a unique head
- Every head heads a unique phrase
- All non-head material is phrasal

Order
- In English, all heads precede complements
- In English, specifiers precede heads
- In English, adjuncts on either side

Optionality
- Non-head material can be optional (depending on the head)
